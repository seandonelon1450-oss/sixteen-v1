Data charter

You own your data.

  1. Your data is yours. We hold it for you, to do work for you. Take it with you any time.
  2. Never sold. Not to advertisers, data brokers, providers, or partners. No ad trackers.
  3. Never used to train models. Not ours, not anyone's. The list of providers is public.
  4. Export in one tap. Everything, in a readable format (PDF + JSON/CSV).
  5. Delete in one tap. Gone from the live system right away, and from backups within 30 days.
  6. Encrypted. In transit and at rest. Device-only rooms we can't read at all.
  7. Collect the minimum. Each feature asks only for what it needs. Every connector is opt-in.
  8. Your choice of home. Our encrypted cloud by default, or on your device only.
  9. People see only what you share. Team access is limited, logged, and visible to you.
  10. No quiet changes. New use of your data? We ask first, in plain words. Silence never counts as yes.

Questions

Plain answers.

Does Arbor use AI?

Yes. When you ask the board a question, AI drafts the sixteen seat voices and the chair's synthesis from the archetype framework, then shows you the finished deliberation to read. Nothing is sent, shared, or decided without your tap — every action waits for you. Your data is never used to train models.

Are the sixteen seats real people?

No. Each seat is an archetype — a named temperament and mandate, like The Allocator or The Standard. No real people's names, likenesses, or endorsements are used. The counsel is illustrative, not anyone's actual views.

What does it cost?

$9.99 a month, $59.99 a year, or $299 once for life. Founding access opens first to the waitlist — joining costs nothing, and nothing is charged until checkout opens.

What happens to my data?

The only personal data Sixteen holds is your waitlist email, which we use solely to invite you to founding access. Export it or delete it any time on your data page. The full charter is above.
